The pitch

What GKOF actually is

The movement is the same discipline you already know from a Gorilla-style game — swing, climb, cover ground using nothing but your arms. What changes is what else is allowed to move that fast.

Every map has one thing hunting in it. It doesn't spawn on a timer and it doesn't reset when a round ends badly for it. Staying ahead of it is the whole game.

First real pathing pass

The hunter now tracks a player's actual route instead of a fixed patrol point. It sounds small, but it means distance from spawn stopped meaning safety.

Recently

Base movement tuning

Propulsion friction is being tuned to feel familiar to anyone coming from a Gorilla-style game, on purpose — the fear should come from the situation, not the controls.

Ongoing

First ambient audio pass

Right now silence is doing most of the work, which is fine but flat — nothing tells you something is closing in, and nothing tells you it left. A real audio pass is next.
