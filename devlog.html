Devlog

What changed, what broke, and what got fixed — in the order it happened. Most of it hits the Discord first; this is the version that gets written down.

This week

First real pathing pass

The hunter now tracks a player's actual route instead of a fixed patrol point. Rooms that felt safe purely because they were far from spawn no longer are — that's staying in, it changes more about how people play than we expected.

Recently

Base movement tuning

Propulsion friction has been adjusted to stay close to what most players already know from a Gorilla-style game. The goal is instant familiarity with the controls, so the fear comes from the situation instead.

Recently

First map layout

A first test layout is playable solo. Too many straight sightlines right now — it favors outrunning the threat over actually losing it. Next pass adds blind branches and dead ends.

Ongoing

First ambient audio pass

Silence is carrying a lot of the tension already, but it's flat — nothing signals that something is closing in, and nothing confirms it's gone. A real audio pass is the next priority.

Ongoing

Quest 2 / 3 / 3S support

Performance is being tested against Quest 2 first, since it's the most limited of the three headsets. Whatever runs well there will run well everywhere else — the reverse doesn't hold.

Most updates land on the Discord first, in real time.
